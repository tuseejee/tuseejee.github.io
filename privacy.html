Moneypenny – Privacy Policy

Moneypenny is a private personal-assistant application used solely by its owner, Mike Navarro, with his own Google account.

It accesses Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive data only to help its owner manage his own schedule, mail and files. That data is processed on the owner's own computers, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except where needed to carry out the owner's own requests.

No other users' data is collected. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
